# Thrive4 Cleaning website refresh

The latest brief replaces the previous Cleannora identity with Thrive4 Cleaning. The home composition is retained, with updated imagery and branding. Maid to Clean was reviewed as a reference for service discovery and booking flow; its copy, prices, reviews, statistics and guarantees are not reproduced.

## Page implementation

- About: full-width photographic hero, three floating service highlights, four-column service strip, three-photo editorial collage, values and next steps.
- Services: image-led directory with working category filters and eleven distinct service photographs.
- Pricing: compact arched photo hero, interactive property controls and readable estimate summary.
- Booking: dark green introduction, step overview and existing six-step validated request flow.
- Areas: local-availability introduction and postcode lookup without invented coverage claims.
- Contact and quote: tailored heroes, contact information and enquiry forms.
- FAQ: centered search-led introduction and searchable questions.
- Careers: professional work photography, requirements and expression-of-interest form.
- Journal and articles: editorial layouts, cleaning guides and related articles excluding the current article.
- Service details: category-specific visual treatments, service scope, inclusions and related services.
- Privacy, terms and status pages retain their appropriate content and truthful draft/status states.

## Imagery

New generated illustrative images are stored in public/images as thrive-australian-kitchen.png, thrive-australian-office.png, thrive-australian-team.png, thrive-window-cleaning.png and thrive-oven-cleaning.png. Models are depicted in Australian settings with European appearance in accordance with the user's latest visual preference. These are illustrative brand visuals, not photographs of verified employees. Cleaning tools, work and clean interiors take priority over portraits. The eleven service entries have distinct asset paths; a listing and its corresponding detail page intentionally use the same service photograph.

## Verification

Local route and responsive checks: qa/thrive.mjs and qa/thrive-report.json. Functional checks: qa/flows.mjs and qa/report.json. TypeScript, existing validation tests and production build were run locally. No publication or deployment is performed.

Live submissions remain unconfigured. Forms validate and show the existing truthful unavailable state; requests are not silently accepted or reported as delivered. Business contact details, coverage and rates remain subject to confirmation.
